About Sanad

Sanad is a professional mural and street artist based in Dahab, South Sinai, Egypt. He specializes in creating vibrant, custom wall paintings and graffiti for camps, hotels, restaurants, and private spaces across the Red Sea region.

His artwork bridges modern urban graffiti culture with traditional Egyptian and Bedouin heritage, resulting in stunning large-scale murals that transform environments.

Selected Works & Murals

  • Underwater and Marine Life Murals
  • Bedouin and Bohemian Wall Decor
  • Custom Graffiti and Calligraphy
  • Large-Scale Facade and Building Painting
